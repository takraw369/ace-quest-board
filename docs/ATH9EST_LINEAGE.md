# ATH9est → Athlete Quest lineage

ATH9est is retained as a legacy code name and internal lineage of **Athlete Quest**, not as a separate public product.

## Confirmed lineage

- **2022**: `ATHLETE QUEST` already existed as a coaching / inquiry concept.
- **2025-12-23**: `ATH9est クエスト設計 ver0.1` developed the concept into a 12-week / 72-quest program.
- Its structure was: Weeks 1–4 activation, Weeks 5–8 **eight trials**, Weeks 9–12 **the ninth = integration → self-direction → return**.
- The eight trials were Recovery, Focus, Prevention, Output, Mobility, Fuel, Emotion, and Relation.

## Current interpretation

`ATH` = Athlete.

`9` = the ninth layer: integration. The goal is not to collect eight isolated abilities, but to integrate them into a self-regulation OS. In Japanese, 9 (`kyu`) also works as a bridge toward the sound/shape of **Q** in Quest; this is a current brand reinterpretation rather than a documented original etymology.

`-est` = superlative suffix, reframed away from comparison with others toward updating one's own best. It can also carry the philosophical echo of Latin `est` (“is”): returning to and acting from who you are.

A useful hidden layer is `Q(U)EST`: **U = YOU**. The system does not complete the Quest; the participant does.

## Brand hierarchy

- **ACE** — education OS / principles
- **Discovery Quest** — discover your patterns
- **Athlete Quest** — transform through observation, adjustment, choice, experiment, and self-direction
- **ATH9est** — internal lineage / 9-Gate integration archetype
- **Quest Board** — implementation UI: Vision > Milestone > Quest > Task

## 9-Gate archetype

1. Recovery
2. Focus
3. Prevention
4. Output
5. Mobility
6. Fuel
7. Emotion
8. Relation
9. Integration

Gate 9 is not another parallel skill. It sits above the other eight and turns them into a personal self-regulation system.

## Product rule

Do not revive ATH9est as a competing public brand. Preserve the name and structure as the origin layer inside Athlete Quest, and evolve the old 72 quests through the current ACE / Discovery Quest / body-awareness / inquiry-centered model.
